Plant Light Meter

Lux & Daily Light Integral for Indoor Plants

iOS 17+ & iPadOS App Store ID: 6813500465 Privacy Verified

Most houseplant care guides recommend "bright indirect light," but what does that really mean? Plant Light Meter turns your iPhone camera into an ambient light analyzer. Lay your device where your plant stands, screen facing the window, and get an immediate, calibrated reading in seconds.

✓
Light Bands & Plant Matching: Instant classification into Low, Medium, Bright Indirect, or Direct Sun with matching species profiles.
✓
DLI (Daily Light Integral) Calculator: Calculate total photosynthetically active radiation over the course of the day for sunlight and LED grow lights.
✓
100% Private Camera Analysis: Uses camera sensor exposure parameters in real time. Never captures, saves, or transmits any photos.
✓
One-Time Lifetime Ownership: Essential measuring is completely free. Optional $3.99 lifetime unlock for spot saving & DLI mode. No subscriptions.
