Restoring In-App Purchases

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?

All TestSoft purchases are permanent lifetime unlocks associated with your Apple ID. To restore:

  • Open the application on your device.
  • Navigate to Settings or tap the lock icon on the unlock screen.
  • Tap Restore Purchases.
  • You will never be charged twice for a lifetime purchase with the same Apple ID.

Plant Light Meter: Lux & DLI FAQ

How should I position my device when taking a measurement?

Place your iPhone flat at the exact location where your plant's foliage receives light, with the screen facing toward the primary light source (such as the window or grow lamp). Allow 1-2 seconds for the optical exposure sensor to stabilize.

Does the app save or upload photos?

No. The camera is accessed purely in real time to read exposure parameters (shutter speed, ISO, and aperture). No photo is ever taken, written to disk, or transmitted over any network.

What is DLI (Daily Light Integral)?

DLI measures the cumulative amount of photosynthetically active radiation your plant receives over a 24-hour cycle (expressed in mol/m²/day). You can input the number of hours the spot receives sunlight or grow light to see the calculated DLI.

Image Compressor Offline FAQ

Are my photos uploaded to a cloud server?

Never. All compression algorithms execute natively on your iPhone's hardware. The app works fully offline in Airplane Mode.

Does compression delete my original photos?

No. The compressed version is saved as a separate copy or exported to your desired destination. Your originals in your Photos library remain intact unless you choose to delete them.
