Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026 · Effective Date: October 8, 2026
Summary: We do not collect your personal data. All TestSoft applications are engineered from the ground up to operate entirely on your device. We do not operate tracking servers, we do not require account logins, and we never sell or share user information.

1. The TestSoft Privacy Commitment

TestSoft ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Hayri Odabaş (testsoftlab.com), builds standalone utilities for Apple iOS and iPadOS. Our fundamental design philosophy is that user privacy is a non-negotiable standard.

2. Data Collection & Tracking

Across our entire application lineup:

  • Zero Analytics: We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Meta SDK).
  • Zero Advertising Trackers: Our apps contain no third-party behavioral advertising or tracking networks.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need to create an account, register an email address, or provide any identity credentials to use our apps.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Some of our utility applications request system permissions to deliver their core functions. In every case, this processing occurs strictly on your device:

  • Camera (Plant Light Meter & Exposure): Used in real time to calculate ambient exposure parameters (lux and light integral). No photographs, video clips, or image streams are ever saved, recorded, or transmitted to any server.
  • Photo Library (Image Compressor & EXIF Remover): Used strictly to read the image you explicitly select for compression or EXIF inspection, and to save the processed result back to your library. Your photos never leave your device.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (Linecast Teleprompter): Used in-memory to synchronize teleprompter text scrolling to your voice. Audio processing is executed locally on Apple's on-device Speech engine.
  • Haptics & Audio (Beatwise & Row Counter): Used for tactile tap feedback and sample-accurate metronome clicks via standard iOS system sound APIs.

4. In-App Purchases & Financial Data

Any optional lifetime pro unlocks or tip purchases are processed directly by Apple Inc. through the App Store using StoreKit. TestSoft has no access to your payment methods, credit card details, or billing address.

5. Apple App Store Privacy Nutrition Labels

In accordance with Apple App Store developer requirements, TestSoft's applications report: "Data Not Collected".
